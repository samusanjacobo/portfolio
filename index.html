Orchestra

Live performance demands precision, awareness and timing. I document concerts with a discreet presence, focusing on expression, coordination and atmosphere. From full ensemble moments to individual musicians, my aim is to translate the intensity and precision of each show into still images. Every concert is approached with respect for the stage, the audience and the music itself. With experience working with large ensembles, I adapt confidently to low-light environments and provide complete visual coverage, from rehearsals to the live show.

Live Music

Music is presence, rhythm and atmosphere. I document performances as they unfold, focusing on timing, expression and the relationship between artists, audience and space. Whether in concert halls or live venues, my goal is to translate the experience of being there into images that feel immediate and authentic. I capture live shows as they happen, reacting quickly to shifting lights, spontaneous interactions and the intensity of the stage. Each frame aims to preserve the raw connection between artist and audience.
